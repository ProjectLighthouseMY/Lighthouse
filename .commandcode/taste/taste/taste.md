# Taste
- Prefers minimalistic, quiet/subdued visual design — especially for a security research site — with generous margins, whitespace, and responsive layouts. Confidence: 0.9
- Prefers subtle, restrained motion over flashy effects (chose the "subtle sweep" animated-beam option over the brighter "realistic beacon" alternative). Confidence: 0.8
- Wants visual elements to stay correctly anchored to their source across resizes; open to — and suggests — SVG/shared-coordinate techniques (e.g., a shared viewBox scene) instead of CSS viewport-percentage positioning when overlays drift on resize. Confidence: 0.6
- Prefers to review and explicitly approve a concrete implementation plan (with summary, test plan, and assumptions) before the agent makes changes; approves plans verbatim ("PLEASE IMPLEMENT THIS PLAN"). Confidence: 0.7
- Prefers concise, evidence-driven short-form research notes (~500–700 words) over long-form deep dives, matching the site's minimal tone. Confidence: 0.7
- Wants research posts to draw on multiple sources and include concrete statistics with explanations of what the benchmarks actually measure (e.g., CyberGym/ExploitGym methodology), not just outbound links. Confidence: 0.6
- Likes research posts to include first-hand evidence from real disclosures, following responsible-disclosure naming practice: keep an affected entity anonymous unless it has acknowledged/accepted the report. Confidence: 0.5
